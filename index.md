# Privacy Policy — PedalEditor

**Developer:** Meaningful Sound  
**Contact:** meaningfulsound (at) gmail.com  
**Last updated:** October 31, 2026

## What we collect, per app

**PedalEditor:** Collects anonymous usage telemetry — which screens open, which features 
you use, which taps lead to a purchase or a dismissal. Routed through TelemetryDeck, 
a privacy-focused analytics service. The data is tied to a one-way-hashed, rotating 
identifier — not your Apple ID, name, email, or any device identifier that can be used 
to track you across apps. We use it to see where our paywall, trial, and onboarding 
break, and to decide what to fix.

**Fretboard Origin and SongForm:** No telemetry, no analytics. These apps do not collect, 
store, transmit, or share any information from your device.

## What we don't collect, across every app

- No accounts. No sign-in. No login system of any kind.
- No advertising. No ad SDKs. No ad identifiers.
- No cross-app tracking. No IDFA. No App Tracking Transparency prompt.
- No personal data — not your name, email, location, or device identifiers.

## Local Network Permission

PedalEditor requests access to your local network solely to enable MIDI communication 
between your iPhone or iPad and a Mac on the same Wi-Fi network. No MIDI data leaves 
your local network.

## iCloud Sync

PedalEditor offers optional iCloud sync of your preset library. This data is stored 
exclusively in your personal iCloud account, encrypted end-to-end by Apple. Meaningful 
Sound has no access to your synced content.

## No Advertising, No Third-Party Sharing

Meaningful Sound does not sell or share data with third parties for advertising. 
TelemetryDeck receives anonymous usage events only and does not share them onward.

## Children

PedalEditor does not knowingly collect data from anyone, including children under 13.

## Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes in a future version of the app, this page will be updated with a new effective date.
