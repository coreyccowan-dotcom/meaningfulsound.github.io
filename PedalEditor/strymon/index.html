What fluency cost

21 manuals. Every parameter table read, every range confirmed, every control cross‑checked against what the pedal actually receives. It isn’t glamorous work — mostly it’s a PDF and a MIDI monitor and a question about whether this knob goes to 127 or to 100. The app holds the answer so you don’t have to.

Facts

Available on iPhone, iPad and Mac. Connects over Bluetooth LE MIDI, Wi‑Fi (RTP‑MIDI) and USB. Bluetooth pairing happens inside the app on iPhone and iPad; on Mac you pair in Audio MIDI Setup.

No account. No analytics. No third‑party SDKs. iCloud sync across your devices at no extra cost, with a local fallback if iCloud isn’t available. Presets, songs and full backups export as files.
