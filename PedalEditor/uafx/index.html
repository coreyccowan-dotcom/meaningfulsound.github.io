What fluency cost

14 manuals. The amp modelers were the first chapter — seven pedals sharing a house style, with the three‑channel cluster (Knuckles, ANTI, Enigmatic) each landing at 77 or 78 controls, which sounds like a coincidence until you’ve read all three. The effects line is more varied: the Galaxy runs concurrent echo and reverb with clock, the Max maps two entirely independent compressor engines, the OX Stomp is a speaker cabinet emulator with close‑mic positioning. Different boxes, same method.

Facts

Available on iPhone, iPad and Mac. Connects over Bluetooth LE MIDI, Wi‑Fi (RTP‑MIDI) and USB. Bluetooth pairing happens inside the app on iPhone and iPad; on Mac you pair in Audio MIDI Setup.

No account. No ads. No cross‑app tracking. Anonymous usage telemetry only — no personal data. iCloud sync across your devices at no extra cost, with a local fallback if iCloud isn’t available. Presets, songs and full backups export as files.
