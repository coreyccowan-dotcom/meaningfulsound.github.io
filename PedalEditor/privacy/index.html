Privacy Policy — Meaningful Sound

Apps: Fretboard Origin, PedalEditor, and associated tools
Developer: Meaningful Sound
Contact: meaningfulsound@gmail.com
Last updated: October 31, 2026

What we collect, per app

PedalEditor: Collects anonymous usage telemetry — which screens open, which features you use, which taps lead to a purchase or a dismissal. Routed through TelemetryDeck, a privacy-focused analytics service. The data is tied to a one-way-hashed, rotating identifier — not your Apple ID, name, email, or any device identifier that can be used to track you across apps. We use it to see where our paywall, trial, and onboarding break, and to decide what to fix.

Fretboard Origin and SongForm: No telemetry, no analytics. These apps do not collect, store, transmit, or share any information from your device.

What we don't collect, across every app

  • No accounts. No sign-in. No login system of any kind.
  • No advertising. No ad SDKs. No ad identifiers.
  • No cross-app tracking. No IDFA. No App Tracking Transparency prompt.
  • No personal data — not your name, email, location, or device identifiers.

Device Permissions

Depending on the app, we may request specific permissions to enable core functionality:

  • Local Network: Used solely for MIDI communication between your devices. No data leaves your local network.
  • Microphone: If applicable, used only for audio analysis (such as tuning). No audio is ever recorded, stored, or transmitted.

iCloud Sync

Some apps offer optional iCloud sync of your content (such as preset libraries). This data is stored exclusively in your personal iCloud account, encrypted end-to-end by Apple. Meaningful Sound has no access to, and does not collect, any iCloud-synced content.

No Advertising, No Third-Party Sharing

Meaningful Sound does not sell or share data with third parties for advertising. TelemetryDeck, our analytics provider, receives anonymous usage events only and does not share them onward.

Children

Our apps do not knowingly collect data from anyone, including children under 13, and are safe for all ages.

Changes to This Policy

Any future changes to this policy will be reflected on this page with a new effective date.
