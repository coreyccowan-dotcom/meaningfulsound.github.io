SongForm

A songwriter's workspace for sections, lyrics, layers, and co-writes.

SongForm is a native iPhone and iPad workspace built around how songs are actually written — not how DAWs want you to write them. Build a song as a sequence of sections (verse, pre-chorus, chorus, bridge), layer recordings and chord performances per section, edit lyrics with Nashville-style formatting, and co-write with collaborators using a fragment-based contribution model that preserves who wrote what.

Songs as Structure

Every song is a sequence of sections. SongForm treats them as first-class objects — reorder, rename, trim, and recolor — instead of burying them in a timeline ruler. Each section carries its own measure count, pickup detection, and trim boundaries.

Layers, Tracks & Recording

Each section can hold any number of layers. A layer is either an audio recording, a chord performance, or a step pattern. Audio captures to WAV or M4A AAC at 44.1kHz with input gain control, a live waveform, peak/average metering, and a count-in. Each recording is tagged with a track type so you can mute the scratch vocal while keeping the bass.

Lyrics & Theory

  • Lyric editor. Section-specific text with Nashville-style indent formatting for chorus and bridge.
  • Lead sheet. Melody-line charting with a chord grid above the words.
  • Chord events. Record chord shapes against a backing track and capture them as performed.
  • Borrowed-chord library. Four factory presets plus custom collections; modal strip shows scale context.
  • 20+ scales. Major, minor, modes, pentatonic, blues, and more — with degree and interval calculations.
  • Sequencer & pads. Step patterns with note mapping, arpeggiator config, filter types.
  • Thesaurus. In-context word suggestion for lyric writing.
  • Tempo & key. BPM-sync'd durations, beat grid visualization, and a metronome with count-in.

Co-Writing with Fragments

SongForm doesn't try to be Google Docs for songs. Songs aren't documents — they're decisions. SongForm uses a fragment model: every lyric line, audio clip, or melodic idea is an atomic, attributed unit with a status (draft, contributed, accepted, dismissed) and a provenance trail.

Lead-as-gatekeeper. Writers submit drafts. The session lead reviews pending contributions and accepts them into the master or dismisses them back to the pool. Leadership can transfer between collaborators at any time, with a full audit trail of who held it when.

  • Atomic fragments. Lyric, audio, or melodic — every idea is tracked, timestamped, and attributed.
  • Revision history. Per-fragment edit log: who changed what, when, with notes.
  • Arrangement proposals. Multiple proposed arrangements; one formal/approved arrangement per session.
  • Split percentages. Contributor-level royalty attribution baked into the document.
  • Share-link invites. Token-based URLs via meaningfulsound.net with a 14-day expiry and revoke control.
  • CloudKit sync. Master song lives in the lead's shared zone; contributors sync their working copies through their own private zones. (Sync engine is in active development.)

The .songform Document

Songs in SongForm are .songform packages — flat-directory bundles containing a song.json definition plus an audio subfolder. Portable: copy through Files.app, AirDrop to a bandmate, attach to email, back up however you back things up. Server-free: a song belongs to you and lives wherever you put it. CloudKit syncing is opt-in glue, not a requirement.

Your songs belong to you. No proprietary cloud format, no platform lock-in. If SongForm disappeared tomorrow, every .songform file on your device is still a readable JSON document next to a folder of WAVs.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is SongForm?

A native iPhone and iPad workspace for songwriters. Sections, layers, lyrics, audio recording, theory-aware chord tools, and fragment-based co-writing.

How does co-writing work?

Every contribution — a lyric line, an audio clip, a melodic idea — is an atomic, attributed fragment. The session lead reviews pending fragments and accepts them into the master or dismisses them back to the pool. Leadership can transfer between collaborators at any time, with a full audit trail.

Why lead-as-gatekeeper instead of live simultaneous editing?

Songwriting isn't a Google Doc. Decisions about what stays and what doesn't belong to whoever the team agrees should hold the lead at the moment. Fork-and-merge with explicit acceptance preserves intent and credit better than chaotic real-time editing.

What is a .songform file?

A flat-directory bundle containing the song's JSON definition and an audio subfolder for recordings. Portable through Files.app and AirDrop. Server-free by design; CloudKit sync layers on top for live collaboration.

What can I record?

Per-section audio in WAV or M4A AAC at 44.1kHz, with input gain, live waveform, peak/average metering, and count-in. Recordings tag as vocal, backing vocal, instrumental, pads, drums, bass, guitar, keys, or scratch.

Is SongForm out yet?

Not yet. The core songwriting workflow (sections, layers, lyrics, audio recording, fragment authoring, local document persistence) is wired and working. CloudKit-backed real-time collaboration is in development for the public launch.

Does SongForm collect data?

No. No accounts beyond your existing iCloud, no analytics, no advertising, no third-party SDKs. Sync uses your personal iCloud, encrypted end-to-end by Apple. Local songs never leave the device unless you choose to share them.
